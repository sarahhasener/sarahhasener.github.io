## Which photos show industrial trawlers / factory ships off the West African coast?

### Takeaway
The best, clearly West-Africa-specific photos of industrial "super trawlers" are in the Greenpeace Media Library from the 2012 Arctic Sunrise West Africa ship tour (photographer Pierre Gleizes): the Dutch super trawler "Afrika" and the Lithuanian super trawler "Margiris" (142 m), both photographed about 30 nautical miles off Mauritania. No free-licensed (Wikimedia Commons) photo of an *active* industrial trawler off West Africa was found; Commons offers pirogue photos (Senegal, Mauritania) and the Nouadhibou ship graveyard instead.

## Which photos are freely licensed vs. press/NGO photos usable with attribution? (incl. artisanal pirogues for contrast)

### Takeaway
Free licenses are available mainly for the *artisanal* contrast images (Wikimedia Commons pirogues in Senegal/Mauritania, e.g. "Kayar – Strand mit Fischerbooten", reportedly CC BY-SA 4.0).
